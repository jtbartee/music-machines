{{NAME}} {{VERSION}} — macOS VST3 / AU / Standalone
===================================================

WHAT'S IN HERE
  {{NAME}}.vst3        VST3 plugin
  {{NAME}}.component   Audio Unit
  {{NAME}}.app         Standalone app, no DAW needed

Universal binary — runs natively on Apple Silicon and Intel Macs.
Requires macOS 10.15 or later.


INSTALL
  1. Copy {{NAME}}.vst3      to  ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3/
  2. Copy {{NAME}}.component to  ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/Components/
  3. Drag {{NAME}}.app to /Applications if you want the standalone.

  In Finder: Go > Go to Folder… and paste the path.


FIRST LAUNCH — IMPORTANT
This plugin is not code-signed or notarized, so macOS quarantines it and
your DAW may show it as missing, damaged, or simply not list it at all.
Clear the quarantine flag once, in Terminal:

  xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3/"{{NAME}}.vst3"
  xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/Components/"{{NAME}}.component"

Then restart your DAW. Logic and other AU hosts may need a full quit and
reopen so the plugin is re-validated; you can check the AU yourself with:

  auval -a | grep -i "{{NAME}}"
